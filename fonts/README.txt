Put the brand fonts here (Google Fonts, OFL license):

- Big Shoulders Display: BigShouldersDisplay[wght].ttf or BigShouldersDisplay-ExtraBold.ttf
  https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Big+Shoulders+Display
- JetBrains Mono: JetBrainsMono[wght].ttf or JetBrainsMono-ExtraBold.ttf
  https://fonts.google.com/specimen/JetBrains+Mono

The pipeline finds the files by the start of the name and applies the ExtraBold weight (800).
