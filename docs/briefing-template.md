# Entry briefing (1 per video, ~2 min of the owner's time)

The briefing is done through **short questions in the chat** (`AskUserQuestion`), each with my recommendation marked; the owner only confirms or changes it. The result goes to `build/<slug>/briefing.md` and acts as the video's contract: the self-review gates check against it (`docs/review-gates.md`).

## When
After the premise test (step 1 of `new-video`), because the briefing options depend on the central question and the register. If the premise does not pass, there is no briefing (propose another topic).

## The questions (4 per `AskUserQuestion` round, 2 rounds at most)
Round 1 (image and sound):
1. **Set:** 2 to 3 places proposed from the premise (e.g. city square, urban park, sidewalk, kitchen, beach), each **with the surroundings it would have in real life** (square: buildings around it) and with layers and people in action. Each option carries **1 reference photo** of the real place (in the option's `preview`, in words) and, when there is one, the candidate ready-made full set (CC0). Recommendation marked; the default is to start from a ready-made set, not from props on the `meadow`. *(Approved later in a still, side by side with the reference; criteria in G4.)*
2. **Camera and POV** *(Blender animation quality depends on this)*: each option states the animation risk.
   - still observer at eye height, with a slow push-in: low risk, because only the camera moves;
   - camera that follows the walking witness: medium risk (calibrated step, no skating);
   - first-person POV of the witness (the camera is the head; if the hands appear, the risk is high);
   - slow orbit (space only).

   Recommendation: observer with a slow push-in and the witness doing 1 simple action in a medium or far shot (no walking in close-up). The choice goes into `briefing.md` and into the `blender-specialist` NOTES; gate G5 (animation) checks against it.
3. **People in the scene:** none, 1 witness, 2 to 4 in the background, light crowd. Recommendation: 1 witness + 2 in the background.
4. **Sound mood:** quiet and tense, lively and natural, dramatic (low end), minimal. Recommendation follows the story; remember that without narration the `sound-designer` always runs.

Round 2 (text, voice and light):
5. **Narration:** no voice (on-screen text) or voice v4 (Chris). *Ask every video; use it to test voice against text on the same topic.*
6. **What the panel's big number measures:** 2 or 3 candidates in plain words (e.g. "weight x1 -> x4.9 million", "meters to you", "people remaining"); recommendation marked; no derived index.
7. **Tone and boldness:** curiosity, consequence or calamity; how strong the closing line is ("would likely collapse" or just "may change").
8. **Light and time:** morning (dawn), noon, evening, night; recommendation follows the story (calamity: light that darkens).

**Limits stay at the default without a question:** 61 to 70 s, render up to ~3 s/frame and no retention hypothesis. They are written at the top of round 2 so the owner can change them through "Other" if wanted (all 9 viral-study changes always apply; the hypothesis is the table of moments in `progress.md`).

Extra art questions only when the premise calls for them (e.g. "does the opening go from small to human?").

## Format of `build/<slug>/briefing.md` (generated by me after the answers)
```
# Briefing: <title> (<date>)
Premise: central question / register (premise.md)
Set: <place> (still approved: <date> | pending) · Light: <time> · People: <how many>
Camera/POV: <observer | follows | first person | orbit>; witness: <action>, <shot>; animation risk: <low/medium/high>
Panel: <what the big number measures> (start -> end) · Rows: <2>
Sound: <mood>; ambience per scene; visible action = its own sound
Narration: no voice | voice v4
Tone/closing: <register>; doubts <= 2
Limits: <s> s, <= <s/frame> s/frame, hypothesis: <...>
```
Decisions the owner did not make keep the recommendation and are marked "(default)".
