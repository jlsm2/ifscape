# Self-review gates (the video only reaches the owner after passing all of them)

I review myself and deliver only the final product. Each gate has an owner, an objective criterion and **up to 2 rounds** of fixes; if it still fails on the 2nd, the video moves on and the problem is listed in the delivery sheet (never hidden). The gates came from the failures of a past video (ants-human-size); every new failure the owner catches becomes a new gate or criterion here, plus a line in `.claude/agents/lessons.md`.

## Common rules
- **Rounds:** fix and repeat the gate, max 2; on the 3rd attempt do not insist: record the residue in the delivery sheet.
- **Cost:** if a fix requires redoing the whole Blender work (> 1 h), stop and show the cost to the owner in one line first (the only exception to autonomy).
- **Freeze:** what the owner already approved (set still, panel phrase) does not change without asking.
- **Log:** each gate round writes one line in `build/<slug>/progress.md`: gate, round, findings, what changed.
- **Parallelism:** G3 runs in parallel; G5 and G6 can run together once `timeline.json` exists.
- **Evidence, not impression:**
  - Each gate finding states: scene, frames, what is seen, probable cause and a label: **confirmed** (seen in the frame or measured), **probable**, **art choice** (not fixed on its own) or **hypothesis**. Only "confirmed" blocks the gate.
  - Before claiming how something behaves, inspect the `.blend` or the real code; do not deduce from the name.
- **Before and after:** every G4 and G5 fix shows the same frame, with the same camera, before and after, in a cheap still (`--stills`). Checking only the fixed frame is not enough: also look at the neighbors (+-6 frames), because a lone frame may be a passing pose.
- **Regression:** changing animation timing, a cut or a scene duration after the `sound-designer` invalidates the sync, and G6 runs again against the new `timeline.json`. After each fix, check whether these came back: skating foot, piece sinking or floating, broken cut, sound out of sync, duplicated object.
- **Not verified is not approved:** a criterion I could not check goes in `delivery.md` as "not verified", with the reason; never as passed.
- **Smallest change:** first fix the defect that breaks reading (order: missing asset -> clipping or floating -> wrong movement -> continuity -> sync -> camera -> polish); do not redo the whole set to fix one detail, nor polish while a HIGH is open.

## Delivery
When G1 to G8 pass (or the residue is listed): `py tools/qa/delivery.py <slug>` generates `build/<slug>/delivery.md` (1 page: what I decided, what stayed weak, what it cost, retention hypothesis, pending items for the owner) and the frame sheet. I answer the owner with: MP4 path, cover, `post.txt` (paste block), a 6-to-10-line summary of `delivery.md` and the question "approve?". Nothing more.
