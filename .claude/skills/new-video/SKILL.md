# New video

One video per session. Details and rationale: `CLAUDE.md` (lean-mode workflow), `docs/script-rules.md` ("Lessons from the viral study" block) and `docs/viral-study.md`. This skill only gives the order and the gates.

## Before starting
- Topic: `research/topic-bank.md` (queue and follower requests, local and optional). Slug in English with hyphens.
- **Reading by moment (replaces the "at most 3 changes" rule):** every video applies the 9 changes of the viral study (`docs/viral-study.md`, section 4). With 1 video per change, a new topic each time and few views, the result could not be attributed; now each change has its own second in the video and the table "moment → change → what to expect on the curve" is noted in `build/<slug>/progress.md` (e.g. event at 8 s → the curve does not drop before 10 s). The reading is on the YouTube retention curve, point by point.

## Autonomous mode
I review myself and deliver only the final product. The owner is called at **3 points**: (1) the **entry briefing** (short questions in the chat with `AskUserQuestion`, each with my recommendation marked; template in `docs/briefing-template.md`; becomes `build/<slug>/briefing.md`), (2) the **set stills sheet + the panel phrase** (step 7), and (3) the **delivery**. In between, the **gates G1 to G8** (`docs/review-gates.md`) run on their own, with **up to 2 correction rounds per gate**; any residue goes on the delivery sheet, never hidden. The only exception to autonomy: a fix that requires redoing the whole Blender (> 1 h) stops and shows the cost in one line. Narration: **ask in each video's briefing** (no voice or voice v4).
**Delivery:** `py tools/qa/delivery.py <slug>` builds `build/<slug>/delivery.md` (gates, cost, frames and audio); I fill in the "FILL IN" parts (decided, came out weak, hypothesis, tokens and Gemini candidates) and reply to the owner only with: MP4, cover, the "PASTE INTO POST" block of `post.txt`, 6 to 10 lines of the sheet and "approve?".

## Order
0. **Entry briefing** (after step 1, because the options depend on the central question and the register): set, light and time, people in the scene, sound mood, narration, what the panel's big number measures, tone and limits. Without a briefing there is no render.
1. **Premise** (`director`, Mode 0, title only) → `premise.md`, with the **central question** ("and us? and the planet?"), the **register** (calamity, curiosity or social consequence) and what the image must not imply. If it fails: stop and propose another topic.
2. **Thesis** in 5 lines + through-line → `thesis.md`.
3. **Fronts by topic:** before researching, write 3 to 6 fronts DERIVED FROM THE TOPIC, not the calamity mold. For each: the question it answers, which scale it covers (body, place, planet), a possible real analog and the **image available on the stage** (assets or an already-made scene). Cut a front with no image and no human scale; include one on **myths** (what the public believes) and, in a reused video, one on **what the set already shows**. Overlapping fronts are split (one topic per agent). After the research, reassess: a front that only produced reserve becomes a note for the next one.
3a. **Source candidates:** look the topic up in `docs/verified-sources.md` (what was already opened is not reopened) and run ONE **Gemini Deep Research** search through the owner's Chrome following `docs/deep-research-procedure.md` (show the prompt and ask the owner before sending; writes `research/<slug>/candidates.md`). The result is HINTS: each `fact-check` opens the URL and copies the literal excerpt; a false candidate is marked and counted. Note in `progress.md`: research tokens, candidates received/verified/false (a measurement that decides whether it becomes the standard).
3b. **Research** (`fact-check`, research mode; **one front per agent, all in parallel** `research-<front>.md` + 1 consolidation with the end-to-end chain; data only enters with a literal excerpt + opened URL) → `facts.md` with sheets (where it lives, permitted verb, scope), PANEL block (curve + threshold ladder + signature number), Consequences, Survival. Primary sources really opened (Chrome if blocked).
4. **Beat map** (skill `beat-map`, no agent) → `beats.md`, with the witness task and the columns where it lives and image before/with/after.
5. **Script** `scripts/<slug>.json`, written beat by beat, **with a humans scene and a planet scene** and without "nobody tested it" as a crutch. Every deadline `stamp` needs a source that states the deadline; otherwise `stamp: null` (the panel disappears). **Stamp and panel only with important information** (sourced deadline, date, counter that measures damage); "SOON"/"LATER" and any vague stamp do not go in.
6. **Script gate (includes the "what the image claims" table per scene and the tone regulator), all in parallel in one message:** `py -m ifscape lint scripts/<slug>.json` + `script-review` + `fact-check` (check). 1 pass; the 2nd only on the changed sentences; stops with no CRITICAL or HIGH. Lint with HIGH blocks.
7. **The owner approves the set STILLS sheet + the panel phrase** before the heavy render (the script passes G3 with no stop for the owner; in ants the empty field was only caught with the video assembled, 1 h of render lost). Before asking: build 3 to 5 set stills (`py -m ifscape blender --stills`), pass G4 (believable floor plan of a place or a ready-made CC0 set, surroundings, 3 layers, people in action, still next to 1 reference photo) and show them; never props scattered over the `meadow`; if a piece is missing, the `blender-specialist` (new piece with 3 actions: transition, looped rest and tremor) or a **whole ready-made set** from a free source (CC0; see `CLAUDE.md`, "Set and assets"). The panel: the big number is understood in 1 s, with no derived index.
8. **Closing always:** every text video ends with an end card (fact + "What should I imagine next?") and the closing sound (ambience cuts at the question, `boom`, low drone). Check it in the render (`sfx.py` does it by itself; look at the last 4 s). **No voice by default:** the script is `"format": "text"`. Only run `py -m ifscape voice` + `tools/qa/voice_check.py` if the owner asks for the narrator version. **Stills 1×** + `art-director` Mode 1 on the contact sheet (checklist: card outside the action, side-by-side ruler, repeated pieces with their own position, tiny piece in profile, close with the opening light), **full Blender render**. With `blender/timeline.json` ready, run the **`sound-designer` ALWAYS** (video without narration; it writes `sfx`, `sfx_auto` and `ambience` into the JSON; after that the script generator no longer overwrites) and only then the assembly `py -m ifscape render`; the `qa` gate runs at the end (peak ≤ -1 dBTP: lower `sfx_lufs` in the script if it overshoots). Animation agents only if something real fails. If only one stretch changed and the scene durations are the same, `blender --frames A-B` redoes only the stretch.
9. **Before asking for approval, look at the video** (`py tools/qa/final_sheet.py out/<slug>.mp4` builds the frame sheet and lists peaks and silences): extract frames (0.3 s, the event, each fact card, the last 4 s) and check for cut-off text, panel hitting the lens, end card legible for ~4 s and the closing sound. **The owner approves the video.** Run `py -m ifscape lint` again: `post.txt` must not contain "OWN CALCULATION" or "[SOURCE PENDING]"; paste into the post only title, description and hashtags (no Sources). Description with no verb stronger than the video's.
10. **After publishing:** commit (no `.env`), `progress.md` with hypothesis and expected result, a line in `research/video-history.md`; analytics and post-audit are MANUAL calls by the owner (`/weekly-analytics`, `/post-audit`); do not run on your own. Failures the owner catches become a line in `.claude/agents/lessons.md`. `/clear`.

## Do not
- No new agent step without the owner's order.
- Reopening an approved video: only for CRITICAL (false fact, platform rule) or the owner's order.
- Cleaning `build/` only when the owner asks.
